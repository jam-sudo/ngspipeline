# Synthetic guide-library reads (GUIDE_COUNT unit test)

20 read pairs, 3 guides (G1, G2, G3), 2 cells. R1 = 16-nt **feature-barcode variant** of a 10x 3' v3 barcode + 12-nt UMI;
R2 = 30-nt TSO + G-padding to the protospacer offset + 20-nt protospacer + guide-A scaffold (layout from docs/decisions/001).
Generated by `tests/data/guide_synthetic/make_synthetic.py`; expected counts are fixed by the design (UMIs per cell x guide):

| cell (GEX barcode) | G1 | G2 | G3 | reads |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A = ACGTAACTCGCACGAC | 5 (4 exact UMIs incl. two duplicated reads, plus one UMI with 1 mismatch) | 3 | 0 | 10 + 1 unknown protospacer |
| B = ACTGATGGTGATTCTG | 4 (protospacer at offsets 30-33) | 0 | 2 | 6 + 1 read with 2 mismatches |

Not counted: 1 read with 2 protospacer mismatches, 1 unknown protospacer, 1 barcode not on the whitelist, 1 barcode with N.
Barcodes in the output are expected as GEX barcodes (kite:10xFB translation). `reads.tsv` lists the design of every read.
